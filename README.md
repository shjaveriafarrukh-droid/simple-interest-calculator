# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
This project provides a simple interest calculator designed to compute the total interest accrued on a principal amount over a given time frame at a fixed interest rate.

## Formula
Simple Interest is calculated using the formula:

$$I = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount (initial sum of money)
- **R** = Rate of interest per year (in %)
- **T** = Time period (in years)
- **I** = Simple Interest earned or payable

## Total Amount Formula
$$\text{Total Amount} = P + I$$

## Sample Inputs & Outputs
| Principal ($P$) | Annual Rate ($R$) | Time in Years ($T$) | Simple Interest ($I$) | Total Amount |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $1,000 | 5% | 2 years | $100 | $1,100 |
| $5,000 | 7.5% | 3 years | $1,125 | $6,125 |

## How to Run / Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate percentage.
3. Enter the time duration in years.
4. Apply the simple interest formula to calculate the interest and total yield.
